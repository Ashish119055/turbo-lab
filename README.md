# Turbo Lab – ટર્બોચાર્જર લેબ

Interactive **3D turbocharger lab** for ITI / automobile trainees in **ગુજરાતી, हिन्दी and English**. Runs in any browser (phone friendly), no install.

Open `index.html`, or the single-file version `dist/turbo-lab.html`.

| Tab | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **3D Lab** | Realistic 3D turbocharger. Accelerator slider drives turbo speed, boost, exhaust temperature, oil pressure and the automatic wastegate (with turbo lag). Cut section with hatched faces, exploded view, part names, air / exhaust / oil / coolant flow in colour, slow motion, sound, glowing hot turbine, guided "How it works" tour. Tap any part for its name, job and material. |
| **Parts & materials** | All 19 parts with job, material and why that material, plus a materials table. "Show in 3D" highlights the part. |
| **Dismantling** | 12-step correct dismantling order with tools and cautions – parts come off in 3D. |
| **Lubrication & cooling** | Animated system diagram: air filter → compressor → intercooler → engine → turbine → silencer, oil feed / drain circuit and coolant circuit. |
| **Failure lab** | 10 faults (oil starvation, dirty oil, foreign-object damage, overboost, wastegate stuck, coking, oil leak, clogged filter, boost leak) with cause, symptoms, what you find and remedy – each changes the 3D model and gauges. Dial-gauge shaft-play check, inspection table and a "find the hidden fault" challenge. |
| **Theory** | Complete simple-language theory: working, compressor & turbine, bearings, wastegate & VGT, types, advantages, disadvantages, turbo vs supercharger, care. |
| **MCQ** | 32 questions in three languages with explanations, including figure-based questions. |

Values and specifications are typical training figures – always follow the manufacturer's manual.

Three.js r149 (MIT, see `js/vendor/three.LICENSE`). `build-single.sh` rebuilds `dist/turbo-lab.html`.
